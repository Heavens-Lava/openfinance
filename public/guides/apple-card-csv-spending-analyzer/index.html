Apple Card CSV guide

Turn an Apple Card export into a private spending dashboard.

OpenFinance reads an Apple Card transaction CSV on your device, then organizes purchases, payments, categories, merchants, and monthly patterns without connecting to your Apple account.

Try synthetic demo dataOpen OpenFinance
Your file remains local: imported CSV contents are parsed and saved in this browser. OpenFinance does not request Apple credentials or transmit the financial contents to an OpenFinance server. Read the details.

How to export and analyze

  1. Use Apple's official Wallet interface to open Apple Card and find the statement or transaction export controls.
  2. Choose a CSV transaction export for the period you want to review. Apple's exact navigation may change, so rely on its current interface and support documentation.
  3. Open OpenFinance, choose Import Data, and select the CSV.
  4. Review detected purchases, payments, amounts, merchants, and categories against the source statement.
  5. Use Categories, Recurring, Cash Flow, and Income & Savings to explore the period.

Expected columns

The current parser recognizes Apple Card CSV fields including Transaction Date, Clearing Date, Description, Merchant, Category, Type, and Amount (USD). If Apple changes the export format, report the column names without submitting real transaction data.

Good fit and poor fit

Good fit

You want an occasional, explicit review of exported Apple Card activity and do not want continuous bank synchronization.

Poor fit

You need real-time balances, automatic transaction feeds, shared household synchronization, or professional accounting records.
